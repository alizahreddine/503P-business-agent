The A Way — University Tutoring Academy

Business identity
The A Way is a fictional university tutoring academy that helps university
students understand difficult course material, plan their study time, and
prepare for assessments.

Mission
The A Way makes structured, approachable academic support easier to find while
being honest about what is and is not currently confirmed.

Services
- Course-focused tutoring and concept review
- Study planning and exam preparation
- Follow-up support for students who express interest
- Academic outreach and teaching-assistant collaboration
- Planning information about sessions and expected tuition

Team
The fictional demo team consists of Maya Haddad, cofounder and academic
programs lead; Omar Khoury, cofounder and operations lead; and a network of
course tutors and teaching assistants. These names are fictional demo facts
for this assignment and do not identify real staff or current assignments.

Unique value proposition
The A Way combines course-aware tutoring support with transparent escalation:
students receive clear answers from documented academy information, while
unconfirmed prices, schedules, tutors, capacity, and availability are labeled
as unavailable or unconfirmed instead of being guessed.

Course catalog
The reusable catalog includes:
EECE 210, EECE 230, EECE 231, EECE 330, EECE 442, CMPS 201, CMPS 203, and
CMPS 211.

Catalog and active offerings
These codes identify catalog courses only. The catalog is not proof that a
course is currently open for registration. No active semester offerings are
confirmed in this local demo dataset. Prices, schedules, tutors, capacity,
registration status, and student enrollment are unavailable or unconfirmed
unless an explicitly verified active-offering record is added.

Student-facing boundaries
This assistant can explain documented academy capabilities and record a
student's interest or feedback in the local demo runtime. It does not perform
official registration, process real payments, access administrator records,
provide financial reporting, or operate student/tutor portals.
